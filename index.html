Dear BINDUSHREE ❤️(PANDA) 💙,

28/07/2006

I know you may not be the type of person who gets emotional easily.
Maybe you will read this and simply smile.
Maybe you will make fun of me for writing so much. 😄
We have laughed together.
We have spent time together.
We have had good moments… and maybe a few not-so-good moments too. 😄
But somewhere between all those ordinary days,
you became a special part of my life.
And honestly…
Sometimes we don't realize the value of a person
until life starts taking everyone in different directions.
**ಒಬ್ಬ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿ ನಮ್ಮ ಜೀವನದಲ್ಲಿ ಎಷ್ಟು ಮುಖ್ಯ ಅನ್ನೋದು,
ಅವರು ನಮ್ಮ ಜೊತೆ ಇದ್ದಾಗ ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ಗೊತ್ತಾಗುವುದಿಲ್ಲ...
ಅವರು ನಮ್ಮಿಂದ ದೂರ ಹೋಗುವ ಸಮಯ ಬಂದಾಗ ಮಾತ್ರ ಗೊತ್ತಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.** 💙
Maybe our lives will change.
Maybe we won't talk every day like we do now.
Maybe we will become busy.
Maybe we will meet new people and create new memories.
But I hope that, somewhere in your heart,
there will always be a small place for the memories we created together.
Because Trust that our friendship not for a time pass.
It is a collection of small moments
that I will remember for a very long time.
The random conversations.
The stupid jokes.
The laughter.
The arguments.
The moments where we did absolutely nothing
but still enjoyed being together. 😄
**ಕೆಲವು ಸಂಬಂಧಗಳಿಗೆ ಹೆಸರು ಬೇಕಾಗಿಲ್ಲ...
ಅವು ನಮ್ಮ ಜೀವನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೊಟ್ಟಿರುವ ನೆನಪುಗಳೇ ಸಾಕು.** ✨
I don't know what the future has planned for us.
But I do know this…
No matter where life takes us,
no matter how much time passes,
if you ever look back at these days,
I hope you smile and think…
**"ಹೌದು... ಆ ಸಮಯ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿತ್ತು, & You think that He is not best but is just my friend."** 💙
And if you ever need me,
even after years,
even after everything changes…
Just remember this:
**ನಾನು ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ ನಿನ್ನ friend ಆಗಿರ್ತೀನಿ.** 🤝💙
Not because I expect anything from you.
Not because you have to do anything for me.
And I trust that you will never break my trust in anything......
So no.....
This is not goodbye.
**ಇದು goodbye ಅಲ್ಲ...
We just starting that new story of Us...** 📖✨
Take care of yourself.
Be happy.
Achieve everything you dream of & I'm always here for you to support for ur dreams.
And whenever life gets difficult,
remember that somewhere in this world,
there is one friend who genuinely wishes the best for you.
**Thank you, BINDU❤️.**
For being a part of my life.
For the memories.
For the laughter.
For simply being you.
